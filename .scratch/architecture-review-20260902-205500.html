  • Strict count guardrail stays internal
  • Multi-pass trimming mechanics stay internal

Solution: Extract pure ES3 serializer and filesystem utilities into isolated modules composed via preprocessor include seams.

  • Leverage: utilities reusable across future scripts
  • Zero global namespace pollution
Top Recommendation

1. Deepen the Slide Placement Engine & 2. Deepen the Audio Marker Extractor

The ExtendScript engine (hostscript.jsx) holds 100% of the timeline risk, marker arithmetic, and Premiere Pro API fragility; collapsing it into deep modules behind small interfaces delivers the highest leverage and immediately unblocks independent testing and maintenance.
